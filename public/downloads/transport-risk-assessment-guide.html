Transport Risk Assessment Guide

IVMS & Journey Management · GCC & India Operations

This guide outlines a structured approach to transport risk assessment for fleet operators serving oil & gas, logistics, and industrial clients. Use it to identify hazards, rate risk, and define controls before incidents occur.

Step 1: Identify Hazards

  • Route conditions — curves, construction, weather exposure, night driving
  • Vehicle type — tanker, heavy haul, multi-axle, age and maintenance status
  • Driver factors — experience, medical fitness, violation history, fatigue risk
  • Cargo — DG classification, load stability, temperature sensitivity
  • External — traffic density, school zones, pedestrian areas, security zones

Step 2: Rate & Prioritise Risk

LevelLikelihood × SeverityAction
CriticalHigh likelihood, severe consequenceStop operation until controlled
HighProbable with significant impactImmediate controls within 7 days
MediumPossible with moderate impactControls within 30 days
LowUnlikely or minor impactMonitor & review quarterly

Step 3: Define Controls

  • Elimination: Reroute away from high-risk zones where feasible
  • Engineering: IVMS overspeed alerts, geofencing, camera monitoring
  • Administrative: Journey plans, rest policies, driver coaching
  • PPE / Emergency: Spill kits, fire suppression, emergency contacts

Step 4: Monitor & Review

Review risk assessments after any incident, route change, or regulatory update. IVMS dashboards should feed into monthly safety performance reviews with documented management actions.
