Driver Management Center (DMC)

End-to-End Driver Lifecycle Management · Case Study Overview

The Driver Management Center is Celeson's integrated framework for managing driver competency, compliance, and performance across large contracted fleets — particularly in India where licence fraud, medical gaps, and fragmented records create systemic risk.

Core Functions

Screening & InductionBackground verification, licence validation, medical fitness
Competency TrainingDefensive driving, certification tracking, reassessment
Real-Time MonitoringIVMS telematics, overspeed alerts, route compliance
Performance RankingDynamic risk score (0–100), coaching & improvement plans
Medical & FitnessPeriodic checks, fatigue assessment, vision tests
Incident ManagementRoot cause analysis, near-miss reporting, learnings
Route IntelligenceHazard mapping, geofencing, high-risk zone alerts
DocumentationDigital driver repository, licence & permit tracking

Expected Outcomes

Organisations implementing DMC frameworks typically see measurable reductions in overspeed violations, improved audit readiness for client HSE reviews, and lower incident frequency — particularly when combined with control room intervention and defensive driving programmes.

Alignment with Motor Vehicles Act 2019

DMC supports corporate duty of care obligations under the amended Act — driver accountability, digital enforcement readiness, and structured competency management for fleet operators.
