Fleet Safety Audit Checklist

Transport Operator Self-Assessment · UAE & India · Predict. Prevent. Protect.

1. Vehicle & Equipment

Current registration, insurance & fitness certificates on file
Preventive maintenance records up to date
Brakes, tyres, lights & mirrors inspected within 30 days
Fire extinguisher, first-aid kit & spill kit present (DG vehicles)
Load securing equipment verified before dispatch

2. Driver Qualification

Valid driving licence verified against RTO / authority records
Medical fitness certificate current
Defensive driving training completed within 24 months
Background verification completed
IVMS / telematics violations reviewed (90-day window)

3. Journey Management

Journey plan filed for long-haul / high-risk routes
Rest stops & fatigue breaks defined and enforced
Route risk assessment updated for known hazards
Pre-trip & post-trip briefings documented

4. Governance & Documentation

Incident & near-miss register maintained
Corrective actions tracked to closure
Contractor safety requirements communicated
Management review conducted within last 12 months
